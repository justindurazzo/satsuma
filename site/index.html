A file converter that lives in Finder’s right-click menu. The new copy lands next to the original, and nothing leaves your Mac.

Download for Mac View the source

Free and open source. macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon and Intel.

What it converts

  • ImagesJPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, TIFF, AVIF, SVG to each other or PDF
  • AudioMP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC, OGG, Opus, AIFF
  • VideoMP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, AVI, plus GIF or MP3 out
  • PDFto Word, images, EPUB, text or Markdown
  • DocumentsWord, RTF, ODT, Markdown, HTML, EPUB to each other or PDF
  • PresentationsPowerPoint and ODP to PDF or images
  • SpreadsheetsExcel, ODS, CSV, TSV, or PDF
  • Subtitles and archivesSRT and VTT; ZIP, TAR and RAR; whole folders to ZIP
  • Quick fixesCompress, trim, rotate, resize, strip metadata, mute video

Install

  1. Download the DMG and open it.
  2. In Terminal, type bash and a space, drag in Install Satsuma.command, then press Return.It adds Satsuma to Applications and to Finder’s right-click menu.
  3. Say yes when it offers to install the converters.Satsuma uses free tools like ffmpeg and LibreOffice, installed with Homebrew.
  4. Right-click any file, then choose Quick Actions, Convert with Satsuma.

Satsuma is a macOS port of Mandarina, the Linux converter by JorgeFTS. Ported by Justin Durazzo. MIT licensed.
